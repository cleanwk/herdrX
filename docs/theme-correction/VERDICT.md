The original F2 recommendation to enable Ghostty minimum contrast 4.5 is withdrawn. Actual native rendering and the [pinned Ghostty shader](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty/blob/82938b633ba646db38591d969c3c526332bd7e65/src/renderer/shaders/shaders.metal#L105) show that it substitutes pure black/white for failing colors and loses ANSI hue distinctions. The revised bounded approach—minimum contrast 1, faint opacity 0.85, semantic app-role contrast, and chromatic preset shading—is appropriate. Explicit 256-color/RGB output and arbitrary foreground/background pairs retain application meaning; their pale samples are not certified as universally readable and do not justify stripping or recoloring application output. No additional regression was established within this scored batch.
