# HerdrX 0.1.25

- Consolidate sidebar controls into one aligned icon row: Hide Sidebar, Activity Filter, Search/commands and New Terminal Tab.
- Align Device Filter and Settings at the bottom left, with New Space and Add Device at the bottom right. Click New Space for quick creation, or right-click for the name/directory form.
- Remove branch and folder icons from Space subtitles. Titles and metadata now share one left edge with consistent spacing.
- Preserve existing shortcuts, filtering, server-owned sessions and terminal actions. Active filters remain visible through selection styling and accessible values.

Validation: [sidebar toolbar checks](../rex-beta-comparison/SIDEBAR-TOOLBAR.md). Native checks cover a 200pt sidebar, Chinese/light and English/dark toolbar rendering, Space/tab creation, search cancellation, device settings and sidebar visibility. Final text-only Space metadata was checked Chinese/light. Full VoiceOver and all-theme coverage remain outside this pass.
