# Pane geometry, 0.1.14

2026-10-12. The user's explicit ACCM measurements supersede the 8pt gutter / 1.5pt Retina outline in the older spacing and corner records.

- Every pane uses a 1pt inside border. Focus changes only its color: theme accent versus foreground at 28% opacity.
- The canvas contributes 3pt outer margin on all four sides; each neighbor contributes 3pt at internal boundaries. Border gaps are exactly 6pt horizontally and 13pt vertically, including the lower pane's 7pt top-border offset.
- Inline titles use 11pt monospaced type, semibold when focused. The title cutout begins 9pt from the pane frame, masks 5pt on both ends, and separates title/context by 5pt. Mask background follows the terminal background.
- The drag target remains 7pt and shows a 1pt accent line during dragging. Terminal views retain their 4pt left/right/bottom inset, and native close/restore hit targets remain 22pt.
- Keep the existing public window-corner adaptation on macOS 26+, continuous outer bottom corners only, and square internal/top/full-screen corners.

`scripts/test-panes.sh` compiles production geometry and tests 1×/2× stroke edges, exact horizontal/vertical border gaps, the 7pt top offset, nested/stacked/grid layouts, split minimums and selective corner paths. The complete current-head CI is linked from [PR #20](https://github.com/cleanwk/herdrX/pull/20). A successful build or geometry test does not establish physical-device or visual acceptance; historical screenshots show the previous metrics and are not evidence for this change.
